How these sample figures are calculated

All multipliers use $5,000/month of burdened direct labour (wages plus employer costs) as the base. Business-only break-even includes $4,000 of overhead that stops with the firm; full break-even includes $5,000 of overhead. That includes $3,000 entered operating expenses, $800 non-billable staff and $1,200 imputed owner non-billable time. Only this business’s share of each expense is included.

Target multiplier = all-cost break-even ÷ (1 − target profit margin). The current 2.30× is the example pricing setting on the same labour basis. Remaining profit = $72,000 unearned signed fees − $36,000 estimated direct costs − $18,000 allocated overhead. It is an estimate before tax, not cash available. No tax rate or financial account connection is assumed.
