Greens lead.
Everything else supports.

A quiet, warm background. Mostly white and sage cards. Forest for the important moments, lime for a single highlight, and a little purple for comparison or emphasis.

61% neutrals25% greens8% lime6% purple

Suggested visual balance, not a rigid formula.

The rule

Purple is an accent.

Use it for one chart series, a small badge, or a small supporting detail. Keep navigation, primary buttons, notices, and the majority of cards in our greens and neutrals.

01 / Palette & purpose

Every color has a job.

Forest and the existing softer greens work together. Lime stays special. Purple never becomes the default surface.

Page background

Warm mist behind the content. White for the app header and most cards. Sage for selected navigation and supporting cards.

Primary & secondary

Forest for primary actions and one key data card. Leaf green for secondary charts and progress. Pale sage for secondary controls.

Highlights & accents

Lime for one standout result. Purple only for a comparison marker or small insight badge. No purple notice banners.

Consistent beyond the cards.
